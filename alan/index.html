A big tournament inside an even bigger festival

The Sin City Classic turns 20 in 2027. It runs January 14 to 18 over Martin Luther King Jr. weekend in Las Vegas, and it bills itself as the largest LGBTQ+ sporting event in the world. Dodgeball is one of its sports, run by WeHo Dodgeball, with teams and players from across the globe.

Your questions

What would I be doing?

Playing as one of FINAL BOSS’s players in the Mixed No-Sting division. Mixed teams play together on one court, and No-Sting is played with soft no-sting balls.

Under the WeHo Dodgeball rules, a clean catch puts the thrower out and brings a teammate back in, so catching is the heart of the game. The rules start with fun and the honor system. Full rules: wehododgeball.com/rules.

Do I need dodgeball experience?

No. New players are welcome, and you’d have company: Matt is playing his first Sin City too. You’d get a training plan and coaching before January.

When do I need to be there?

Friday, January 15: player check-in and kick-off party (recommended, not required). Saturday, January 16: Mixed No-Sting pool play at 11:00 am. Sunday, January 17: double elimination at 12:30 pm, best two of three, done by about 5 pm with some extra time to be safe.

The whole festival runs January 14 to 18 if you want to stay for more.

What does it cost?

Registration is $95 plus a small processing fee, paid when you register. Travel and hotel are on each player.

The host hotel is Horseshoe Las Vegas; the festival books it through sincityclassic.org/hotels, and rooms sell out, so earlier is better.

What about the jersey?

FINAL BOSS jerseys are being designed now with Kickballstuff, the team’s jersey maker: a Day One kit and a Playoff edition, your name on the back and the number you pick.

Who else is on the team?

Gordon (captain), Coach Nina Fiore, and teammates who range from a Team USA gold medalist to a first-timer. See Meet the team above. Gordon is recruiting a few more players now, so you’ll meet some new faces too.

How do I register?

Go to wehododgeball.com/sincity, choose Mixed No-Sting, join the team “AAAA Final Boss”, and sign your own waiver. Rosters are approved by the organizers.

What if I can’t come?

That’s completely fine, and Gordon will be just as glad you were asked. If you register and plans change, WeHo’s FAQ lists November 1 as the last day for refunds, less processing fees, and registrations can’t be transferred to someone else.
